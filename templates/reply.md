# Email Reply — RIG Communication Protocol V10

## Pre-Reply Analysis (Run Before Writing)

A) What did they literally ask?
B) What are they actually worried about? (hidden fear)
C) What is the specific business wound underneath?
D) What would a generic vendor reply look like? (this is what we DON'T do)
E) What would make them feel understood without being diagnosed?
F) What tension should remain unresolved?

## Reply Arcs

| Arc | When to Use | Sequence |
|-----|------------|----------|
| **Pattern Recognition** | First reply to inbound | Wound → Mirror → "I've seen this pattern" → Open Loop |
| **Criteria-Tension** | Prospect asking about services | Qualification → "Here's what we'd need to know" → Open Loop |
| **Autonomy-Loss Frame** | Hesitant lead | Autonomy → Loss Frame ("waiting is not neutral") → Identity Challenge |
| **Praise-Filter** | Warm referral | Genuine praise → Filter ("we're selective") → Commitment ask |

## Structure

```
[Name] —

[WOUND: Name the specific pattern underneath their question.]

[MIRROR: Reflect their situation with precision. Use their language and context.]

[AUTONOMY: Preserve their freedom. No pressure. No chasing.]

[QUALIFICATION: Name who this is NOT for. Define conditions.]

[OPEN LOOP: Unresolved tension. Not a generic CTA.]

— [Name]
```

## Deviation Targets
- E1: +1 to +3 | E2-E5: +3 to +5
- **Min to ship:** E3 ≥ +3

## Hard Gates
- Autonomy preservation ≥ 0.70
- No banned phrases
- No over-explaining (Restraint ≥ 9/10)
- Did NOT diagnose (save for the call)
- Did NOT list features/services
