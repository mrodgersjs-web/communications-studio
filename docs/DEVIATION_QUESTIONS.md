# The 50 Deviation Engine Questions — Full Battery

These are forcing functions, not brainstorming prompts. Each question pushes the AI writer into a specific deviation corridor that median thinking cannot access. Every question maps to a signal layer and a scoring metric in the master formulas.

---

## STAGE 1: SIGNAL DETECTION (Questions 1-10)
Purpose: Force the system to find ideas the writer would never think of on their own.

| # | Question | Signal Layer | Metric Feeds |
|---|----------|-------------|--------------|
| 1 | What industry best practice has the weakest evidence base but the strongest consensus? | S1: Orthodoxy Hardening | OrthodoxyDestructionDepth |
| 2 | What pain is your target audience complaining about in forums but no thought leader is addressing on LinkedIn? | S2: Wound Frequency | WoundResonanceScore |
| 3 | What new term appeared in the last 90 days that nobody has defined sharply yet — and what should it actually mean? | S3: Vocabulary Drift | SemanticInversionNovelty |
| 4 | What institutional failure pattern keeps recurring but has never been given a name? Name it now. | S4: Villain Emergence | VillainClassSpecificity |
| 5 | What claim did a major voice make this month that they provided zero evidence for? What would the evidence actually show? | S5: Proof Gaps | FalsifiableBetStrength |
| 6 | What LinkedIn post format has been copied so many times it is now invisible? What format has never been tried? | S6: Format Exhaustion | FormatNovelty |
| 7 | What framework from behavioral science, physics, military strategy, or game theory has never been applied to this industry? | S7: Adjacent Collision | CrossDomainTransferScore |
| 8 | What is the most expensive invisible cost in your audience workflow that they have normalized as just how it works? | S2: Wound Frequency | WoundResonanceScore |
| 9 | If you had to bet 10000 dollars on one prediction about this industry in the next 12 months what would it be and why? | S5: Proof Gaps | FalsifiableBetStrength |
| 10 | What would a hostile outsider — someone who thinks this entire industry is a scam — say is the most damning truth? | S4: Villain Emergence | EnemySpecificity |
