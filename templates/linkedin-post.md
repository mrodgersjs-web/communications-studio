# LinkedIn Post — RIG Communication Protocol V10

## 8-Step Structure

```
1. HOOK (Line 1): Pattern interrupt. Stop the scroll. Rupture expectation.
2. WOUND MIRROR (Lines 2-3): Name the industry pain nobody says out loud.
3. AUTHORITY FRAME (Lines 4-5): Why you have the right to say this.
4. MECHANISM (Lines 6-10): The framework, model, or insight.
5. EVIDENCE (Lines 11-13): Specific data, named companies, real numbers.
6. DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION (Line 14): Force the reader to self-assess.
7. OPEN LOOP (Line 15): Leave something unresolved.
8. CTA (Line 16): Invite engagement that's about THEM, not you.
```
