The 5-Phase Dopamine Engine

The median (0σ) is what ChatGPT produces when you say "write a professional email." RIG communications operate at +5σ to +22σ above that baseline by producing five consecutive expectation violations that stack into a compound neural response.

Phase 1: WOUND
Name the specific, expensive pattern they're living inside but haven't articulated. Not "many companies struggle" — but the exact pattern costing THIS recipient money. Gate: precision ≥ 0.60.
Phase 2: MIRROR
Reflect their reality with enough precision they feel seen. Not handled. Not diagnosed. The test: they should think "How did they know that?" — not "That's a nice thing to say."
Phase 3: AUTONOMY
Explicitly preserve their freedom to say no. 10 phrases are banned — "just checking in," "following up," "would love to chat" — because 53 peer-reviewed studies confirm they trigger psychological reactance (r ≈ .20). Gate: ≥ 0.70.
Phase 4: QUALIFICATION
Flip the frame. You are evaluating THEM, not begging for attention. Name who this is NOT for. Force an identity choice. They need to earn the next step.
Phase 5: OPEN LOOP
End with unresolved tension — a diagnostic question, a cost-of-inaction frame. Never a generic CTA. The Zeigarnik effect does the work: incomplete tasks create persistent cognitive tension.
